At the end of 2025 I stepped away from contract work to focus on one product: **Celixion**.

Celixion is a system that turns messy maritime brokerage emails into structured, queryable data. Brokers live in inboxes. Ships, cargos, and match suggestions get lost in threads. The goal is to replace that manual routine with something clearer and faster.

### What I'm building

- Parsing pipelines for brokerage emails
- AI-supported classification prompts
- A Supabase-backed data model for ships, cargos, and matches
- Internal dashboards brokers can actually use day to day

### Stack

React, Next.js, Node.js, Supabase, and modern AI tooling — chosen for speed of iteration more than novelty.
